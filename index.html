Why Mooki?

Three things we keep in every carton, from the farm to your fridge.

100% REAL

  1. Fresh milk first

    Mooki is made from fresh cow's milk, not reconstituted powder. The chocolate and fruit flavors go in without covering up the milk.

  2. UHT sterilization

    The milk is heated to a high temperature for a few seconds, then sealed airtight. That is what keeps Mooki shelf stable without added preservatives.

  3. Four flavors, one character

    Choco Blast, Creamy Vanilla, Strawberry Pop and Banana Bounce. All four taste best cold, straight from the carton.
